## Inspect the retained layout

![Later console source shell with a disclosure banner, empty side panes and central canvas; application scripts and services disabled](../visuals/operator-console-static-desktop.png)

This is a real browser capture of a sanitized, script-disabled rendering of the later console's retained HTML/CSS. It shows its dark palette, left navigation/list area, central canvas and right detail panel. It is **not a working application capture**. Blank panes are intentional; no example contacts, tracks, sensor feeds, synthetic frames or model output were manufactured or loaded.

For this documentary rendering:

- The application script was removed; fixtures and services were not loaded or started.
- All controls were disabled. Their presence does not mean they were tested.
- The original LIVE label was replaced with STATIC, and the engine-frame claim with a no-frames-loaded label.
- A disclosure banner and explanatory watermark were added outside the original data content. The capture is therefore not an unmodified historical screenshot.
- A restrictive content policy blocked scripts and connections. No external resource elements were present in the inspected preview.

The original source files were not changed or distributed. Only this screened capture and documentary account are included here. Observed capture dimensions and transformations are recorded in the [visual inventory](../visuals/README.md).

## What is reusable for a documentary showcase

The existing palette, typography and separation of navigation, primary visual material and supporting details are useful presentation references. A non-operational results reader can apply those general patterns while keeping an artifact's origin, date, limitations and evidence category visible.

This does not call for copying the operational application script, fixtures, data services or processing logic into the public showcase. Nor should an empty interface shell replace an actual recorded result or be advertised as a live demo. The capture is an expandable supporting visual, not the project's primary proof.

## Keep the evidence categories separate

- Original test summaries remain historical declarations from their recorded versions; they were not rerun for this review.
- The later vision bridge remains a separately documented synthetic scenario, not a live camera connection or field result.
- The source-shell capture demonstrates a retained layout only. It demonstrates neither of those test categories.
- The drone project's selected-frame observations and training results belong to that separate study, not to this interface capture.

## Availability

The public repository remains documentation-only. There is no hosted operator console, live sensor connection, model inference, scenario playback or executable application package here. No operational code, credentials, private source links, location data, model weights or deployment instructions were added.
