03 / Playback

Source support
is not acceptance.

Source-supported
The application source supports all 91 discrete timesteps, t=0 through t=90. The six-bookmark restriction belongs only to the separate review adapter (offline-fixture mode), not to the application’s full-timeline capability.
Recorded behaviour
Historical synthetic-test summaries are retained. No complete playback recording or newly verified full-runtime result is presented here.
Timing and endpoint
Playback waits for each response. Ninety scenario seconds need not equal ninety viewing seconds. The source currently loops; stopping at t=90 remains a product decision.
